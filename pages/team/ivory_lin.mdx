Ivory is an undergraduate student majoring in Actuarial Science at the University of Hong Kong, with a minor in Mathematics. She joins the HIVE Lab during her semester exchange at the University of Toronto. Drawn to the Life & Health branch of actuarial industry, she unlocks her academic potential in Public Health and Biostatistics fields. Her current project sheds light on homelessness prevention and housing system planning. To strengthen the statistical and mathematical foundation for related study, she develops a long-term interest in Causality and Machine Learning. She plans to study nonparametric inference about counterfactual causal effects next year.
