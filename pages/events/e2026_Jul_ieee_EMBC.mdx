# HIVE Lab Research Presentations at IEEE EMBC 2026

<img src="/events/ieee-embc-2026.png" alt="HIVE Lab Research Presentations at IEEE EMBC 2026" />
---
Five HIVE Lab papers will be presented at IEEE EMBC 2026 in Toronto from July 28 to 30.

The projects cover stroke risk prediction, antimicrobial peptide design, medical question answering, cancer mortality prediction, and identifying social determinants of health in clinical notes.

Congratulations to our presenters: Hemn M. Khdr, Fang Sheng, Amir Hosein Haji mohammad rezaie, Javier Mencia Ledo, and Zhenxiao Yang from Institute of Health Policy, Management and Evaluation Dalla Lana. 
The flyer includes the paper titles and presentation times. Stop by if you are attending. We would be glad to discuss the work and connect.
