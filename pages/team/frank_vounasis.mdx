Frank Vounasis is a graduate student in Health Systems Research (Health Informatics emphasis) at the Institute of Health Policy, Management and Evaluation, University of Toronto, supervised by Dr. Zahra Shakeri. His research examines patient-centred accountability in the governance of artificial intelligence in Ontario academic hospitals. Frank brings public-sector experience in cybersecurity strategy and risk management to questions of how health systems govern emerging technologies. He holds an ALM in Systems Engineering from Harvard University, an MHA from Cornell University, and an Honours BHS from York University.
